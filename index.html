Robotics Car Programme – Decision Brief

For school management · Whether to purchase robot cars, and how many

The Choice

Training is by staff from Big Dipper Studio, the only official provider of the UBTECH cars used in these competitions.

Buying at least 4 cars is the minimum for the taught course. Buying 16 cars adds the Yuen Long hosting package. Fewer than 4 cars is equipment only, with no training.

The Two Car Types

Students learn hardware and programming. Both types can enter competitions.

Custom (改裝車)

  • 3D-print and change hardware only
  • Electronic parts cannot be changed
  • Focus: building and modifying

Original (原裝車)

  • Keep the car as supplied
  • Write programmes and choose strategy
  • Focus: coding and problem-solving
